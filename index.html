How the Dependent Fee Calculator Works

The Saudi dependent fee (also known as the family levy) was introduced in July 2017 and increased gradually over several years before reaching its current level.

This calculator estimates the total fee based on:

  • The number of dependents sponsored under your iqama
  • The date your iqama expired (or last expiry date)
  • The date you plan to renew your iqama until

Fees are calculated only for periods after 1 July 2017. Any time before that date is automatically excluded from the calculation.

The calculation is an estimate and may differ slightly from official systems due to rounding rules or specific renewal conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When did the dependent fee start in Saudi Arabia?

The dependent fee was introduced in July 2017. No dependent fees are charged for any period before this date.

How much is the dependent fee per month?

The fee increased gradually over time and reached SAR 400 per month per dependent in July 2020.

Is the dependent fee charged if my iqama expired before 2017?

No. If your iqama expiry date was before July 2017, fees are only calculated from 1 July 2017 onward.

Is this calculator official?

No. This website is not an official government service. It provides an estimate based on publicly available information to help with planning.

Can the calculated amount differ from Absher or official systems?

Yes. Official systems may apply different rounding or billing rules depending on the renewal duration and individual case.
